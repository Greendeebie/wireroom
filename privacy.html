Privacy

Wire Room does not collect anything about you. There is no account, no telemetry, no analytics, and no server belonging to Wire Room for your information to be sent to.

What is stored, and where

Your settings — the feeds you have added, your symbols, board style, height, threshold and any API keys you have pasted in — are stored in a plain file on your own computer, at %APPDATA%\WireRoom\settings.json. Recently fetched commodity prices are cached beside it so the app does not re-request a daily price every few minutes.

Both files stay on your machine. Nothing in them is transmitted to the developer, and deleting the app's data folder removes them completely.

What the app connects to

Wire Room only contacts servers you have told it to contact:

  • The news feeds you add. Wire Room ships with none. Every feed on your board is one you entered, fetched directly from that publisher.
  • A market data provider, if you enable the prices panel. Depending on which you choose in Preferences, that is Yahoo Finance, or Twelve Data and Alpha Vantage using API keys from your own free accounts. Your API keys are sent only to the provider that issued them.
  • Nothing else. No usage reporting, no crash reporting, no update checks.

When you click a headline it opens in your normal browser, and from that point the publisher's own privacy policy applies.

What those third parties see

A request from Wire Room to a feed or a price provider carries the same information any request carries: your IP address and the app's identifier (WireRoom/1.0). Wire Room does not add anything to it. Those services have their own privacy policies, which govern what they do with it.

Children

Wire Room is a general-audience utility. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
