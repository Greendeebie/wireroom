Getting started

Adding your first feeds

Wire Room arrives empty and fetches nothing until you add a source. Everything on your board is something you chose.

It takes about twenty seconds

  • Right-click the board and choose Preferences
  • Under Wire Sources, paste a feed address into the second box
  • Give it a short label — that is what appears on the board when a story comes from it
  • Press Add

Stories from every feed are pooled, scored, and shown in order of severity rather than order of arrival.

Finding a feed for a site you read

  • Look for an RSS link in the site's footer, or on its about or help page
  • Try adding /rss or /feed to the site's address
  • Search for the site's name plus “RSS feed”

Two or three beats ten

Ten feeds do not make the board ten times more useful. Most outlets carry the same agency copy, so a long list mainly buys duplicates. Two or three sources you trust, plus the severity threshold, gives a quieter and more useful board than a wall of everything.

If nothing appears

  • Check the threshold. Set it to 1 to see everything, then raise it until the board is as quiet as you want.
  • Check the address is the feed, not the page. A feed usually ends in .xml, /rss or /feed, and looks like unformatted code in a browser.
  • Give it a minute. Wire Room fetches on a timer rather than instantly.
  • Preferences reports any feed it could not read, with the reason.
